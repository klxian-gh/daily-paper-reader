## 摘要
我们利用一系列计算化学方法，提出了一种形成 DNA 和 RNA 核心核碱基前体的简单且高效的路径。苯在以 N2 或 CO2 为主的早期大气中具有热化学稳定性，可能通过高层大气光化学或地表闪电形成，并在早期地球或火星上积累。然而，将氮插入苯环以形成嘧啶和嘌呤被广泛认为具有挑战性。我们提出，氮的引入是通过 HCN 对苯 π 体系的 1,4-环加成，随后进行 C2H2 碎片化机制实现的，这一过程已得到量子化学计算的证实。该路径可能由海洋表面的光化学作用或局部储层上的偶发撞击事件促进，可导致嘧啶的形成，嘧啶可进一步与 NH3 和 HCN 反应生成嘌呤。将该路径扩展到早期火星，我们的光化学模型模拟了在寒冷、干燥的地表条件下杂环化合物的形成，这些条件有利于高浓度的苯和 HCN，但缺乏液态水。因此，我们提出在干燥阶段形成的有机物可能在随后的湿润阶段溶解到地表水中，并作为海洋沉积物富集。这一结果支持了火星采样返回计划，该计划重点关注可能保留生命起源前特征的古代水环境。

## Abstract
We propose a simple and efficient pathway for the formation of precursors to core nucleobases in DNA and RNA using a suite of computational chemistry methods. Benzene, which is thermochemically stable in N2- or CO2-dominated atmospheres, could have formed via upper-atmospheric photochemistry or surface lightning and accumulated on the early Earth or Mars. However, nitrogen insertion into the benzene ring to form pyrimidine and purine is widely considered to be challenging. We propose that nitrogen incorporation occurred through HCN 1,4-cycloaddition to benzene's π-system, followed by a C2H2 fragmentation mechanism, as confirmed by quantum chemistry calculations. This pathway, potentially facilitated by photochemistry at the ocean surface or episodic impact events on local reservoirs, can lead to pyrimidine formation, which can further react with NH3 and HCN to produce purine. Extending this pathway to early Mars, our photochemical model simulates heterocyclic compound formation under cold, dry surface conditions that favor high benzene and HCN concentrations but lack liquid water. We thus propose that organics formed during dry phases may have later dissolved into surface waters during wet phases and become concentrated as ocean sediments. This result supports Mars Sample Return efforts focused on ancient aqueous environments likely to retain prebiotic signatures.
